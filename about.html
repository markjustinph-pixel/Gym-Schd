About This App

MJM Workout Planner brings your weekly workouts, daily tasks, and nutrition goals together in one place. Sign in to customize a personal workout plan, save your macro goal, and manage your profile.

Plan your training

Arrange exercises across the week and keep your workout schedule easy to follow.

Track your routine

Use daily tasks and workout progress tools to stay consistent. These are currently saved in this browser.

Personalize nutrition

Set an individual macro target and use the food log to keep an eye on your day.

Accounts and private settings use Supabase. The site can be hosted on Vercel. Account sign-in becomes available after the project credentials and database policy are configured.
